# Whale
### The messaging app built for groups that have outgrown flat chat

Whale is a next-generation messaging platform that combines the simplicity people expect from chat with the intelligence, organization, and reliability that growing groups actually need. Where traditional apps give every group the same flat thread — DMs, group chats, and not much else — Whale gives every group a space shaped around how it actually communicates, backed by AI that does the remembering, searching, and organizing so people don't have to.

---

## 1. AI — Ask, Search, Summarize, Rewrite, Remember

Whale is AI-native, not AI-bolted-on. Every conversation comes with a set of intelligent capabilities available at any moment:

- **Ask W** — a single assistant that already has context on every conversation you're part of. Ask it a question in plain language and it answers from across your chats, with sources, instead of making you dig for the answer yourself.
- **Search, everywhere** — find anything, in any conversation, by describing what you're looking for rather than remembering the exact words someone used. Search inside one chat or across everything at once.
- **Summarize** — catch up on a busy chat in seconds. Whale distills any conversation into a short, clear recap, tuned to the kind of group it is.
- **Rewrite** — polish a message before you send it. Make it more professional, friendlier, shorter, or translate it entirely, in one tap.
- **Memory** — Whale keeps context on the conversations you choose to share with it, so its answers get smarter and more relevant over time instead of starting from zero with every question. You decide exactly which conversations Whale is allowed to learn from.
- **Turn talk into action** — Whale notices when a message sounds like a commitment or a decision and offers to turn it into a task, so nothing important gets lost in the scroll.

Every one of these lives directly inside the conversation — no separate app, no copy-pasting into another tool.

---

## 2. Bands & Sub-Bands — Groups, Organized

A **Band** is Whale's home for any group — a family, a class, a team, a creative crew, or a public community. Bands come in purpose-built categories, each tuned to how that kind of group actually talks:

- **General** — for any group that doesn't need a specialized structure
- **Family** — built around the rhythms of a household
- **Class** — organized around a course, assignments, and a cohort
- **Studio** — for creative collaborators and projects
- **Team** — built for work, decisions, and follow-through
- **Channel** — public, discoverable communities

**Sub-Bands** are where Whale really departs from traditional chat. Instead of forcing a growing group to fracture into a maze of splinter chats every time a new topic comes up — each one needing its own invites, its own missed messages, its own confusion about who's actually in which chat — Whale lets a Band branch into focused Sub-Bands underneath it. Everyone stays in one place, organized by topic, with nothing to re-invite and nothing to lose track of.

**Channels** extend this further into public communities: a discoverable space with its own handle, open to anyone, joinable by request, or available to follow for announcements-only updates — giving creators, organizations, and communities a native way to build an audience inside Whale rather than bolting one on separately.

---

## 3. Whale Tunnel — File Sharing That Just Works

Sharing a large file over chat is one of the most common — and most consistently broken — experiences in messaging today. Videos get compressed into mush, transfers fail halfway through, and a dropped connection means starting over.

**Whale Tunnel** is Whale's answer: a dedicated, resilient transfer pipeline built specifically for large files.

- **Resumable by design** — if a transfer is interrupted, closing the app or losing signal doesn't mean starting over. Reopen Whale and the transfer picks up exactly where it left off.
- **Built for real file sizes** — full-quality video and large attachments move reliably, without the compression and size caps that plague traditional chat apps.
- **Control over every share** — set an expiration date, limit a file to a single download, or protect it with a password before you send.

It's the difference between "I'll just email it instead" and file sharing that's actually trustworthy enough to use.

## 4. Message Scheduling

Whale lets you write a message now and have it delivered exactly when it should be — later today, tomorrow morning, or on a recurring schedule.

Scheduled messages are delivered reliably and on time, independent of whether your phone is on, your app is open, or you even remember it's coming. Set it once, and it happens — no screenshots, no manual reminders, no messages sent at midnight because that's when you happened to be thinking about it.

---

## 5. Actions — Everything That Needs You, In One Place

Across five or ten active conversations, the things that actually need a response get buried fast. **Actions** solves this by pulling everything that's waiting on you into a single, unified feed:

- Tasks assigned to you, due soon
- Messages you've scheduled that haven't gone out yet
- Files about to expire
- Mentions you haven't seen yet
- Decisions your group has recently made

Whale's AI helps populate this automatically — surfacing likely tasks and action items from your conversations — but you're always in control: nothing gets added without your confirmation.

## 6. Whale vs. Traditional Chat Apps

| What you're trying to do | Traditional chat apps | Whale |
|---|---|---|
| Find a message from weeks ago | Scroll endlessly, or search one thread at a time | Ask in plain language, across every chat, and jump straight to it |
| Catch up on a busy chat | Read hundreds of messages, or skip them and miss what mattered | Get a clear summary in seconds |
| Keep a growing group organized | Splinter into new group chats, re-inviting everyone each time | Branch into Sub-Bands — one group, fully organized |
| Build or join a public community | Rely on a separate app bolted onto your messaging | Native Channels with handles, discovery, and flexible join options |
| Send a large video or file | Watch it get compressed, capped, or fail outright | Whale Tunnel — reliable, resumable, full quality |
| Send a message for later | Improvise with reminders and screenshots | Schedule it natively, delivered exactly on time |
| Know what needs your attention | Piece it together from scattered notifications | One Actions feed, always current |

---

## 7. Privacy

Privacy in Whale isn't an add-on setting — it's the default. Every conversation, every Band, and every file is private by design: visible only to the people who belong there, and to no one else.

AI capabilities are entirely in your hands. Every conversation lets you decide exactly what Whale's AI is allowed to do with it — from full assistance to none at all — so intelligence never comes at the cost of control. Whale is built on the principle that people should get the benefits of AI-native messaging without ever wondering who else can see their conversations.

---

## 8. The Impact

Messaging has quietly become the operating system for how people actually run their lives — families coordinating schedules, teams making decisions, classes organizing around a semester, communities forming around shared interests. But the tools haven't kept up. Every major chat app has spent the last decade adding surfaces — DMs, groups, stories, channels — without ever solving the underlying problem: conversations that matter get lost, groups that grow become unmanageable, files that should just send don't, and nothing keeps track of what still needs to happen.

Whale is built on a simple bet: the next generation of messaging shouldn't just move text faster — it should actually understand the conversation, keep it organized as it grows, and make sure nothing important slips through. Not a smarter inbox bolted onto chat, but chat rebuilt from the ground up around how people and groups actually communicate — so that the app does the remembering, and the people using it get to focus on what the conversation was actually about.
