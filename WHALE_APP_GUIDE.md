# Whale — App Guide

## Install
1. Open the download link on your Android device.
2. Tap **Install** (if prompted, allow installing from this source — this is expected for a pre-release build).
3. Open the app once it finishes installing.

## Log in
Use the demo account, already set up with sample conversations, Bands, and content:

- **Username:** `demo`
- **Password:** `WhaleDemo2026!`

(Or tap "Already have an account? Log in" → "Create one" to make your own.)

## What to try
- **Waves** — your chats list: DMs and Bands, organized and ready to open.
- **W tab** — ask a question in plain language; it answers from across your conversations.
- **Inside any chat** — try searching, summarizing, or rewriting a draft before sending.
- **Create a Band** — see the categories (General, Family, Class, Studio, Team, Channel) and how Sub-Bands keep a large group organized without splintering it.
- **Send a large file** — see Whale Tunnel handle it smoothly, even on a spotty connection.
- **Schedule a message** — set it for later and see it land in **Actions** until it sends.
- **Actions tab** — everything waiting on you in one feed: tasks, scheduled sends, mentions, decisions.

## If you run into an issue
This is an early build, so on rare occasions the app may show a blank white screen when reopened. If that happens:

1. Close the app fully (swipe it away from your recent apps).
2. Reopen it from the home screen — it will load normally.

**To avoid this altogether:** sign out from the Profile tab before closing the app each time you're done. Logging back in with the credentials above will always load cleanly.
